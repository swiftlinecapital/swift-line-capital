Who We Are

Built by Investors, for Investors

At Swift Line Capital, real estate investing came before lending. Before we ever closed a loan for a client, we were closing deals ourselves — fix and flips, rentals, short-term properties, you name it. Lending became the natural next step because we kept running into the same problem every investor faces: capital that moves too slow for real opportunities.

That experience gave us something most lenders don't have — a seat on both sides of the table. We know what it feels like to find the right deal and need funding yesterday. We know how frustrating it is to sit across from a lender who doesn't understand the numbers, the strategy, or the urgency.

Today, our team holds approximately 80 properties across the US, and we've self-funded every single one using the same products we help our clients access — business lines of credit, real estate loans, 0% interest credit card stacking, and more. We don't just recommend these programs. We use them ourselves.

That hands-on experience is what allows us to get creative. Whether you need a fix and flip loan, a DSCR rental loan, business capital, or a way to access $200K at 0% interest with no income docs, we've likely already solved that exact problem in our own portfolio — and we can help you solve it too.

CEO & Founder
Meet the CEO

Brett Watts

Chief Executive Officer — Swift Line Capital

Brett Watts built Swift Line Capital around a simple principle: the best lending advice comes from someone who has actually done the deals. A seasoned real estate investor based in Charlotte, NC, Brett has built a portfolio spanning short-term rentals, rent-by-the-room, mid-term rentals, and long-term holds across multiple markets — and financed all of it creatively, using the same products his team now helps clients access.

His focus at Swift Line Capital centers on partner relations, marketing, and developing funding strategies that give clients more options than they thought they had. Whether it's structuring a bridge loan to close fast, layering 0% credit lines to fund a rehab, or finding the right business capital product for an operator looking to scale — Brett has likely run the same play himself.
