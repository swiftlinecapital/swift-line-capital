About Swift Line Capital

We exist to provide fast, flexible funding for real estate investors and business owners who are tired of waiting for traditional banks.

Our Story

Swift Line Capital was founded with a simple mission: to serve the underserved lending market. We recognized that real estate investors and business owners needed access to fast, flexible capital without the red tape of traditional banking.

Traditional banks move slowly and apply cookie-cutter criteria that don't work for investors with non-traditional income, those doing fix & flip projects, or entrepreneurs building multi-generational wealth. We decided to build something different.

Starting with a focus on hard money loans for real estate investors, we've grown to offer a comprehensive suite of lending products. Today, we serve over 2,500 clients and have funded more than $40 million in loans across the United States.

Our team brings decades of combined experience in real estate, finance, and small business lending. We understand your challenges because we've lived them. We know what it takes to grow a real estate portfolio, scale a business, or fund a transformational project.

We're committed to maintaining the speed and flexibility that made us different in the first place. Fast approval, competitive rates, and financing solutions designed for your success, not just our risk profile.
