Application Questions

What credit score do I need to apply? +
We work with applicants across all credit profiles. While a higher credit score helps, we focus on your overall financial picture, business cash flow, and collateral. Many of our clients have rebuilt or average credit.
How long does approval take? +
Our goal is 24-hour approval for most applications. The actual timeline depends on how quickly you provide requested documents and the complexity of your application. Simple cases may be approved within hours.
Are there application fees? +
No application fees. No fees to apply or during underwriting. You only pay fees that are disclosed and agreed upon in your final loan terms.
Can I apply if I'm self-employed? +
Yes, self-employed individuals and business owners are welcome to apply. We'll need your tax returns and bank statements to evaluate your application.
