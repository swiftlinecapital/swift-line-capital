About Us

Who We Are

Swift Line Capital is a Charlotte, NC-based private lending brokerage helping real estate investors and business owners access capital fast. We specialize in hard money loans, fix-and-flip financing, DSCR rentals, ground-up construction, bridge loans, and business credit solutions.

We're a lean, high-performance team that values execution, communication, and integrity. Every person on our team owns their work, communicates clearly, and moves with urgency. If that sounds like you, you'll fit right in.
