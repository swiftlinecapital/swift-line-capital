How It Works

Zero-Cost Capital — Strategically Stacked

Credit card stacking is the strategic process of applying for multiple business credit cards simultaneously to maximize your total 0% introductory credit limit. When done correctly, this gives you access to tens — or hundreds — of thousands of dollars at zero interest, structured as a revolving business credit facility.

Swift Line Capital manages the entire process — from selecting the right card portfolio for your profile to timing the applications to maximize approvals and minimize hard inquiries. Most clients receive between $50,000 and $200,000 in available credit.

💳 0% Interest for 12–18 Months

Every dollar you draw is completely interest-free during the introductory period — giving you true zero-cost capital to deploy into deals or operations.

🚫 No Income Documentation

No bank statements, tax returns, paystubs, or proof of income required. Approval is based on your personal credit profile — not your business financials.

🔄 Revolving Credit Lines

Unlike a term loan, business credit cards are revolving — use them, pay them down, and reuse the available credit throughout the 0% window.

🏗️ Perfect for Real Estate Deals

Through liquidation processes, leverage cash off your credit limits to use towards down payments, closing costs, advertising, and more — zero-cost capital deployed directly into your deal.

💼 Works for Any Business Type

Real estate investors, contractors, service businesses, e-commerce — if you have a business entity and a strong credit profile, you can qualify.

📈 Build Business Credit

Every card you open and manage responsibly adds to your business credit profile — setting you up for even more capital down the road.

Requirements

What It Takes to Qualify

  • ✓ 720+ FICO credit score (all three bureaus reviewed)
  • ✓ No bankruptcies, foreclosures, or major derogatory marks
  • ✓ Low credit utilization (ideally under 30%)
  • ✓ No recent late payments (24+ months clean)
  • ✓ Active business entity (LLC, Corp, or partnership)
  • ✓ No bank statements, tax returns, or collateral required
  • ✓ Personal guarantee from the business owner
