Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our financing options and process.

What credit score do I need? +
We work with applicants across all credit profiles. While higher credit scores help, we focus on your overall financial picture, business cash flow, and collateral. Many of our clients have rebuilt or average credit.
How long does approval take? +
Our goal is 24-hour approval. Most applications are approved within 24 hours of submission if all required documents are provided. Simple cases may be approved within hours.
Are there application fees? +
No. We don't charge application fees or fees during underwriting. You only pay fees that are disclosed and agreed upon in your final loan terms.
How long until I receive funding? +
Funds are typically disbursed within 24 hours of closing. For some products like hard money, funding may occur 5-7 business days after closing to allow for final documentation.
Can I apply if I'm self-employed? +
Yes. Self-employed individuals and business owners are welcome. We evaluate applications based on business cash flow and will request relevant financial documents.
Do you finance out of state? +
Yes. We provide funding across all 50 states. Whether you're in New York, California, Texas, or anywhere in between, we can help.
What's your interest rate? +
Rates vary by product, loan amount, term, and your financial profile. Contact us for a personalized rate quote based on your specific situation.
Can I pay off early without penalty? +
Most of our products allow early payoff without penalty. We'll confirm this in your specific loan terms.
What documents do you need? +
Basic requirements include valid ID, business ownership proof, 2 years of tax returns, 3-6 months of bank statements, and a business overview. Specific requirements vary by product.
Do you work with startups? +
We work with startups on a case-by-case basis. We typically require business plans, owner experience, and some initial operating history. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
