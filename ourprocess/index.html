Creative Funding Strategy

Creative Funding = 100% Financing

Here's how we stack products to cover every dollar of your deal.

Option A

True 100% Fix & Flip Loan

Some of our lender partners offer true 100% financing on fix and flip deals — covering both the purchase price AND the rehab budget. Qualification is based on the deal, your experience, and exit strategy. No down payment required.

Option B — Most Popular

Fix & Flip + 0% Credit Stacking

Our most popular gap funding strategy. We help qualified borrowers access $50,000-$200,000 in 0% interest business credit cards (valid for 12-18 months). Use that revolving capital to cover your down payment, rehab overage, or holding costs — interest-free.

Option C

Fix & Flip + Gap Funding

When a lender covers 80-90% of the deal, we bridge the gap with a secondary product: a business line of credit, personal loan, or working capital facility layered on top to cover your down payment and closing costs.

Every deal is different. We look at your project, your profile, and your goals — then find the cheapest path to 100% financing.
