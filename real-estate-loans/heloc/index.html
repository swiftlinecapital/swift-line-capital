Why a HELOC

Stop Leaving Equity on the Table

Every property in your portfolio represents potential capital sitting idle. A HELOC turns that equity into a flexible, revolving credit tool — available when you need it, interest only when you draw.

🔄 Revolving Access to Capital

Draw funds as you need them, repay, and draw again — without applying for a new loan each time.

💸 Interest Only on What You Draw

You only pay interest on the portion of the line you actually use — not the full credit limit.

🏡 Primary or Investment Property Options

We offer HELOCs on both primary residences and investment properties — giving you more ways to access the equity you've built.

⚡ Our Digital HELOCs Fund as Fast as 3 Days

1st, 2nd, or 3rd position digital HELOCs — fully remote and can fund in as fast as three business days.

🛠️ Renovation Capital

Fund rehab projects on existing properties without disrupting your primary financing or draining reserves.

🗺️ All 50 States

We are nationwide lenders and service all 50 states. If the deal makes sense, we can help.

Qualification Requirements

What We Need to Approve Your HELOC

  • ✓ 620+ FICO credit score
  • ✓ Sufficient equity in the property (typically 20–30%)
  • ✓ Non-owner occupied investment or rental property
  • ✓ Recent appraisal or BPO to establish current property value
  • ✓ Clean title and no existing liens beyond first mortgage
  • ✓ LLC or individual borrower accepted
  • ✓ No income verification required in most cases
