The Referral Process

Have a Client Ready to Get Funded?

Here's how the process works once you have a client.

1

Submit the Referral Form

2

We reach out to the client and have them complete an onboarding form. This gives us everything we need to start their funding process - their goals, business needs, credit situation, and more.
