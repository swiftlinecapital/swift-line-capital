Overview

Ground-up construction loans finance the development of new buildings or structures from the foundation up. These loans are designed for developers and builders who need comprehensive financing covering all phases of construction from land acquisition through completion.

Key Benefits

Covers full project timeline

Progress-based draws

Flexible milestone-based funding

Developer-focused underwriting

Interest-only payment options

Conversion to permanent financing

Who This Is Best For

Developers, builders, commercial projects, residential construction, mixed-use developments, and complex building projects.

Application Timeline

Approval typically within 48-72 hours. Draws disbursed based on construction progress.
